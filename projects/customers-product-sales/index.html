Customer & Product Sales Analysis

An early business intelligence project that combined customer and product sales data into an interactive Power BI dashboard.

Power BI, Power Query, DAX, JSON · Coursework project · 2023

Download my original PowerPointView project files on GitHub

What I built

  • Imported two JSON datasets and cleaned customer fields in Power Query.
  • Created measures for total, average, minimum and maximum sales.
  • Compared annual sales, product categories and countries, with customer and product rankings.

What the analysis showed

The original report displayed approximately 29M in sales, with bikes accounting for 96.46% of sales. These figures describe the coursework dataset, not employer revenue or business impact.

Skills demonstrated

Data preparation, measure definitions, visual analysis, dashboard design, and communicating findings for business decisions.

The downloadable presentation preserves my original coursework. The dashboard image is a preview; the Power BI file and supporting materials are available in the repository.
